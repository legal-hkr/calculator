Calculator is a private calculator for Android that calculates exactly: 0.1 + 0.2 is 0.3, and 1 ÷ 3 × 3 is 1. It has no access to the Internet, asks for no permission at all and stores nothing outside the phone: no account, no ads, no analytics.

Calculator:
- The whole expression on one line, with a cursor, and its result previewed as you type
- Exact decimal arithmetic; irrational results computed to as many digits as you scroll to, up to 1,000
- A result with more digits ends with an ellipsis; a finite one is shown in full
- Mathematical notation: 1 ÷ 2π is 1/(2π), and 200 + 10% is 220
- Scientific functions in degrees or radians: trigonometric functions and their inverses, logarithms, powers, roots, factorial
- The last result carries over to the next operation; copy and paste use plain text

Programmer:
- HEX, DEC, OCT and BIN at once, words of 8, 16, 32 or 64 bits, signed or unsigned
- AND, OR, XOR, NOT, NAND, NOR, shifts and rotations; overflow wraps, with a note
- A sheet of bits to toggle one by one

Converter:
- 14 categories, from length and mass to data, fuel consumption and frequency
- Exact factors from the units’ definitions; US and imperial units listed separately
- Unit search by name or symbol in English and Polish, or all units of a category in one list

Widget:
- A calculator on the home screen: the four basic operations, percent and sign change, with the app’s exact arithmetic and a live preview
- Each widget keeps its own calculation until cleared; three rows high, or two with a single-line display

And also:
- History for Calculator and Programmer modes, with day groups, reuse, copy and delete; or no history at all, with nothing kept after the app closes
- Decimal places, scientific notation, the thousands separator and key vibration
- Backup and restore of settings and history to a file you choose
- English and Polish, independent of the system language
- Material 3 Expressive design with your phone’s colors
